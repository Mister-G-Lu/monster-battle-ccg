How to play
  • Each round you gain crystals and draw a card. Deploy creatures from your hand (they can't attack the turn they arrive).
  • Creatures fight the lane directly opposite. If that lane is empty, they strike the enemy commander instead.
  • Reduce the enemy commander's HP to zero before they reduce yours. A blow that destroys a creature carries its excess damage through to the enemy commander. Stalemates after 20 rounds go to whoever kept more of their HP — ties go to the wilds.
  • Elites and bosses have powers that fire on their turns — read them before you commit to a lane. Every boss also Gathers Power: every third turn it grows and strikes you directly, so turtling behind walls loses — win fast.
  • Every first victory lets you recruit a new card. Every boss slain grants +2 max Vitality for the rest of the campaign.
