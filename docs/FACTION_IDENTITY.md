# Faction Identity — Monster Battle CCG

Design reference for the card-identity redesign. Generated by
`scripts/redesign_cards.py`; the source of truth for card stats is
`csv_data/all_card_config.csv`.

## The five factions

Every faction is defined by **pace** and **orthodoxy** (how much it breaks the
rules of fair combat). Each has a distinctive keyword *feel* so two decks never
play the same way.

| Faction | Archetype | One-liner | Signature feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| **War** | Aggro / Tempo | Win the fair fight with a bigger, better army | Disciplined commander |
| **Fortune** | Fast / Rush | Blitz them down before the coin flip catches up | Gambling rogue |
| **Balance** | Control / Defense | Shut down everything they try to do | Patient archmage |
| **Nature** | Defense / Growth | Survive, grow, outlast | Unstoppable living wall |
| **Chaos** | Disruption / Combo | Break the rules of the board | Trickster demon |

```
         FAIR (honest combat)
                WAR
                 |
     FORTUNE     |     NATURE
  FAST ──────────+────────── SLOW
                 |
     CHAOS       |
                BALANCE
         UNFAIR (breaks rules)
```

- **X-axis = pace**: Fortune → War → Chaos → Nature → Balance (fastest → slowest)
- **Y-axis = orthodoxy**: War (most honest) → Nature → Balance → Fortune → Chaos

## Keyword pools (monsters)

Only keywords the offline battle engine actually simulates for monsters are
used. A handful of consume-only or unimplemented keywords (`chance`, `draft`,
`silence`, `entangle`, `heal`, `heal_all`, `flying`, `magic_aoe`, `opportunity`,
`repair`, `breaker_all`, `disease_all`) are deliberately avoided on monsters so
the redesign is genuinely playable, not just flavor text.

| Faction | Primary attack | Signature / utility keywords |
|---|---|---|
| **War** | melee, some ranged | `trample`, `thrash`, `rally`, `charge`, `cleave`, `breaker`, `counter`, `demoralize`, `thorns` |
| **Fortune** | ranged, melee, some magic | `counter`, `reflect`, `stealth`, `drain_crystal`, `charge`, `breaker`, `reach`, `mshield` |
| **Balance** | magic, some melee | `shield`, `mshield`, `reflect`, `antimagic`, `counter`, `cautious`, `boost`, `thorns` |
| **Nature** | melee | `regenerate`, `thorns`, `aggro`, `reach`, `shield`, `boost`, `counter` |
| **Chaos** | melee, some magic | `disease`, `disarm`, `demoralize`, `drain_crystal`, `stealth`, `thorns`, `counter` |

### Faction "signature" keywords

Each faction leans on one mechanic nobody else owns, so the *feel* is distinct:

- **War → `trample`** — a melee hit also splashes the enemy support row (a
  cavalry charge). War is the only faction that sweeps the back row.
- **Fortune → `drain_crystal`** — gain crystals when you destroy an enemy
  (plunder). Fortune literally ramps by winning fights, its "fast" identity.
- **Balance → `mshield` / `antimagic`** — suppress and shield against magic.
  Balance is the only faction that can shut down the board's spellcasting.
- **Nature → `regenerate`** — survive a lethal hit at 1 HP once, plus `aggro`
  taunts that force enemies onto its tanky front line.
- **Chaos → `disease` / `disarm`** — poison the board and strip enemy weapons,
  so their deck stops working.

### Note on "consume-only" keywords

`silence`, `entangle`, `heal`, `heal_all`, `chance`, `draft`, `damage`,
`unsummon`, `destroy` are implemented only for **consume cards** in the offline
engine, so they are kept on the 15 consume cards (which remain `kind=all`)
rather than on monsters. `Fr.Silence` expresses its "silence" identity through
`antimagic` (Magic Suppressed) for the same reason.

## How rarity works now

Rarity buys **specialization, not raw stats**:

- **Common / Rare** — efficient baseline bodies with one obvious job.
- **Epic** — trades 1 attack for an extra keyword.
- **Legendary** — trades 2 attack for the fullest keyword package, but never a
  bigger body.

Monster HP is a **pure function of cost and level** (+1 per level, capped per
cost tier), so no rarity tier ever has a strictly better body than a cheaper
tier. This removes the original pattern where a Legendary was just "the same
card with +HP and +attack" (Kingmushrhum, Calamity, Whiterabbit were all flagged
for this).

## How level-ups work

A level-up now **adds or upgrades a keyword**, not just +1 HP:

- Level 1: primary attack + the creature's signature keyword.
- Mid levels: the second signature unlocks (rare+).
- Max level: full identity, with signature values raised.

Example — Crocodime (Fortune):

| Level | HP | Powers |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 5 | melee 1, counter 1 |
| 3 | 7 | melee 1, counter 2, **drain_crystal 1** |
| 5 | 8 | melee 1, counter 3, drain_crystal 2 |
| 7 | 9 | melee 1, counter 4, drain_crystal 3 |

## Equipment & armor

Items keep their base identity (a Short Sword is still a melee weapon, a
Captains Shield still rallies) but each **faction variant** layers on that
faction's twist, so the six copies of an item are no longer identical:

| Item | war | fortune | balance | nature | chaos |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short Sword | melee, **rally** | melee, **drain_crystal** | melee, **mshield** | melee, **thorns** | melee, **disease** |
| Chain Mail | **rally** | **drain_crystal** | **mshield** | **thorns** | **disease** |
| Battle Axe | cleave, **rally** | cleave, **drain_crystal** | cleave, **mshield** | cleave, **thorns** | cleave, **disease** |

## Counterplay texture

So matchups feel real, not just symmetric:

- **Balance** (silence/antimagic) > **War** (rally army) — shut off the buff before it snowballs.
- **War** (breaker/trample) > **Nature** (armor/taunt wall) — pierce and sweep.
- **Nature** (taunt + thorns + regenerate) > **Fortune** (fragile rush) — outlast the burst.
- **Fortune** (stealth + reflect) > **Balance** (targeted denial) — hard to lock down.
- **Chaos** (disease/disarm) > **War/Nature** (buff-and-heal reliant) — strip their engine.

## What was changed vs. what was preserved

Changed (the identity pass):
- Monster `p1n..p3v` (keywords/abilities) and HP (now cost+level only).
- Equipment/armor `p1n..p3v` (faction twist added).

Preserved verbatim:
- Card IDs, groups, levels, names, types, quality (rarity), kind (faction),
  cost, score, flags, res_path, deck_limit.
- The 3 tutorial/boss cards (`flags=0`: Triglodite Brute, Gerbip king,
  Vibranium Armor).
- All other CSVs (compose, upgrade, resolve, adventure, pve, lang) — their
  `card_id` references remain valid.
